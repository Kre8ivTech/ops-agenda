Design system — v1.0

Everything the product is allowed to look like.

One neutral ramp, one signal colour, three status colours. Two typefaces. Eight-pixel radii and a single elevation. If a screen needs something that isn't here, it needs a decision — not a new value.

Weights

400 body, 600 labels and nav, 700 emphasis inside sentences, 800 headings and buttons. 900 is reserved for numerals in mono.

Line length

Body copy caps at 62ch, supporting text at 44ch. Every paragraph gets text-wrap: pretty.

Mono earns its place: timestamps, IDs, priorities, counts, hex values. Never for prose.

Rules

Labels are 800 and always visible — no placeholder-only fields. Placeholders show a real example, never a restatement of the label. Errors name the cause, not the rule.

06

Status & priority

Colour carries meaning here and nowhere else.

P1 P1 AT RISK P2 P3
Synced Degraded Pending

dot 8px · ring 0 0 0 3px wash

Outlook sync is degraded. Two accounts stopped returning calendar data at 4:12 AM.

Tomorrow's brief will include the new Vendors module.

All 14 items handled. Nothing is at risk before noon.

Meaning

Green is settled or on track. Red is at risk and needs a person today. Blue is informational and never urgent. Grey is inert. A screen with three reds has failed the user before they read a word.

Flagged because the vendor replied twice with no owner assigned. Evidence quotes always sit behind a 2px rule and never carry colour.

Cards

White on paper, 1px border, radius 8. A card never nests inside another card — use a rule instead.

Rows

A flagged row carries a 3px left border in its status colour. Handled rows drop to a plain border — never a strikethrough, never grey text.

Every flagged item must say why it is flagged, in one sentence, in the user's words.

09

Voice & motion

Say what happened. Move only to explain.

Write this

Procurement is the only thing at risk before noon.

Two accounts stopped returning calendar data at 4:12 AM.

Marked handled. It will drop off tomorrow's brief.

Not this

Oops! Looks like something needs your attention 🎯

An error occurred. Please try again later.

Great job! You're all caught up!

Motion

150ms ease for state changes, 220ms for panels entering. Nothing animates on load, nothing bounces, nothing loops. If motion isn't showing a cause and effect, remove it.

Hover is a colour change only — no lift, no scale, no shadow growth.
