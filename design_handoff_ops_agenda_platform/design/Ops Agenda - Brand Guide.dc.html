Brand guide — v1.0

One loop, closed every morning.

The mark holds three things at once: the day as an open loop, a check for what's handled, and an ascent that carries the A of Agenda straight through the loop and out the other side. Everything approved lives on this page.

01

Construction

One radius, one stroke weight, one 55° axis.

The loop

A 31-unit ring at 11.2 weight — the working day. It opens at the bottom right and is cut, never overlapped, where the ascent crosses it.

The check

Rises on the 55° axis through the centre and stops 2.6 units short of the ring's inner edge. That hairline is the mark's only optical breathing room — keep it.

The ascent

The A runs parallel to the check, one weight below it, and its legs land flat on a shared baseline. Breaking out of the loop is the point — never tuck it back inside.

Scale the mark, never the weight. Every element is 11.2 of 99 units wide.

Minimums

Mark alone: 24px tall on screen, 7mm in print — below that the optical gaps close up. Full lockup: 108px wide. Below that, use the mark.

App icon and avatar: mark centred at 62% of the tile, never the lockup.

05

Misuse

Four things that break it.

✕

Don't stretch or condense. Scale proportionally.

✕

Don't recolour the parts. The mark is one colour, always.

✕

Don't rotate. The baseline is always level.

✕

Don't put signal green on ink. Reverse to paper instead.

Where the day is at risk

03 / 18

Slide masters

Two masters only: ink section opener, paper content. Mark top-left at 12mm, slide number in mono bottom-right.

Ink values

Ink · #16201B · CMYK 31/0/16/87

Signal green · #25724D · CMYK 68/0/32/55

Paper · #F7F7F2 · CMYK 0/0/2/3

Confirm a spot match with the printer before any run over 500 — do not let them substitute a stock green.

Stock & finish

400gsm uncoated for cards, 120gsm uncoated for letterhead. No lamination, no spot gloss, no foil, no rounded corners. The brand is matte.

Production

3mm bleed, 5mm safe margin, mark never smaller than 7mm. Supply vector artwork with the logotype outlined — never a raster of the mark.

10

Social avatars

Mark only. The lockup never survives the crop.

Circular — X, LinkedIn, Slack

Ink is primary. Paper only where the platform frame is already dark.

Square — GitHub, YouTube

Radius 23% for rounded platforms, hard square where the platform doesn't crop. Green only for campaign accounts.

Safe area

Export square, design for the circle. Mark at 62% of the tile, inside a 12% inset — the ascent must never touch the crop.

48 64

Avatars stop at 48px. Below that platforms serve a favicon — use favicon.svg, not a downscaled avatar.

Export

One 1024 × 1024 PNG master per variant, sRGB, no transparency — platforms composite transparent avatars onto white and the reversed mark disappears.

Never

No lockup, no initials, no photograph, no seasonal recolour. A person's account uses their face; the company account uses the mark.
